Case intake

Which car is this, and how sure can we be?

Upload a photo. SEMEVIA finds the vehicle, names its make, model and year among 196 categories, and reports only as much identity as the evidence supports.

  1. 1
    Detect

    YOLO11n finds cars, buses and trucks; the largest one is the case.

  2. 2
    Identify

    A fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 plus classical shape, edge, colour and texture features score 196 categories.

  3. 3
    Evidence

    A calibrated Passport keeps every category the evidence cannot rule out, and stops at family or make when it must.

      Nearest training references

      The three nearest of 588 curated training photos (3 per category), by cosine similarity of the model's embedding. Similarity is not a probability, and a neighbour is not proof of identity.

        04 · Evidence Challenge

        Which part of the photo is the model relying on?

        Blurs one of six regions of the crop at a time and measures how the margin between the two leading categories changes. This tests the model's sensitivity, not which car part identifies the make.
